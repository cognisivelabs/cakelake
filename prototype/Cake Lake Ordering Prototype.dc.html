Our story

One oven, then four.

Cake Lake started in 2019 in a Marina kitchen with one deck oven and a Friday-only order sheet. The sheet filled up. Then it kept filling up.

Everything is still baked the morning it's collected, and every cake is finished by hand. The only thing we automated is the waiting — which is why the tracker exists.
