# Cake Lake Bakery — Questions for you

A few things we need your input on before we finalize the design and start
building. No wrong answers here — where you're not sure, just say so and
we'll figure out the best default together.

## 1. Brand

- Is "Cake Lake Bakery" the final name, or a working title? If final, can
  you share your logo files (or tell us if you need one designed)?
- Do you have brand fonts or colours already, or is this a clean slate?

## 2. Menu / catalogue

- What are the main categories of what you sell? (e.g. birthday cakes,
  wedding cakes, cupcakes, cheesecakes, everyday bakes, cookies — or
  different groupings if that's not how you think about your menu)
- For each item: name, price, a description, a photo, and any allergen or
  dietary info (eggless, vegan, gluten-free, nut-free, etc.)?
- Do any items need a minimum lead time to order (e.g. wedding cakes 72
  hours ahead)?
- Roughly how many items total are we cataloguing?

## 3. Delivery

- At launch, do you want delivery, or should the website only handle pickup
  orders to start (with delivery added later)?
- If delivery is in at launch: which areas do you deliver to today?

## 4. Payment

- Do you already have a UAE trade licence and business bank account set up?
  (This affects how quickly we can get you approved with a payment
  provider.)
- Beyond card payments, do you want to offer a "buy now, pay later" option
  like Tabby, or keep it to cards only for now?

## 5. Promotions

Which of these do you actually want to run (pick as many as apply)?
- A straight percentage or amount off (e.g. "20% off")
- Buy-more-save-more deals (e.g. "buy 6, get 2 free")
- Free or discounted delivery above a certain order size
- Seasonal campaigns (Eid, National Day, Ramadan, etc.)
- A discount for a customer's first order
- A referral reward (friend gets a discount, you get credit)
- Bulk/corporate discounts for large or office orders

## 6. Future branches

- Do you have a timeline for opening a second location? Even a rough guess
  (this year, next year, no plan yet) helps us decide how much to build for
  multiple branches now vs. later.
- When you do open more branches, should customers be able to pick which
  branch they're ordering from on the website?

## 7. Running orders day-to-day

- If a customer wants to cancel or change an order after placing it, what's
  your policy — a time cutoff, case-by-case, no changes allowed?
- Who updates an order's status (placed, baking, decorating, ready) as it
  moves through the kitchen — will staff tap a button on a screen, or do you
  already use a kitchen/point-of-sale system we should connect to instead?

## 8. Photos and content

- Do you have professional photos of your cakes we can use, or will we need
  to arrange a photoshoot?
- Who's your point of contact for reviewing text, prices, and photos before
  the site goes live?
